In a rural village in Wayanad, Kerala, the story begins by focusing on two people who were born and brought up in the same place: K. Shankar and Shadevan.

Even though they grew up together, their points of view are different. Even though both believe they are communists, K. Shankar believes in communism as equality, while Shadevan uses communism for power.

When they were students, Shankar was the one everyone loved because of his love toward everyone, and Shadevan was second every time. Even during elections, the first name people suggested was Shankar.

Even though Shadevan was very powerful because of his anger, his ability to fight, and his courage, Shankar won over opponents with words, while Shadevan won with physical power and rage.
